FIRST AID BOOKING SETUP
1. In Calendly, find your Exodus – Emergency First Aid & CPR/AED Training GROUP event and click Copy link.
2. Open public/first-aid-booking.js. Paste the FULL copied URL inside the empty quotes for EXODUS_FIRST_AID_CALENDLY_URL.
3. Deploy the complete folder to Vercel as before.
4. Visit /red-cross.html and click SELECT TRAINING DATE & REGISTER. Test a booking and confirmation email.

This version does not collect government IDs, issue certificates, or claim instructor approval. It keeps the existing security training pages and backend files unchanged. The former Supabase-based request form was removed from the public First Aid page to avoid an unreliable parallel registration flow.
